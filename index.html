What are chequing and savings accounts?

Choosing the right account depends on your needs, goals, and money management style.

Chequing account

Best for everyday needs. Receive your salary, pay bills, make in-store purchases with your debit card, and transfer money between accounts with ease.

Browse our chequing accounts

Savings account

Designed for saving toward a major goal — like a holiday or a new car — or to build a financial cushion for life's unexpected expenses.
